# README.md — Envy Salon DXB Homepage Redesign

## 1. Website Overview

**Envy Salon DXB** is a women-only beauty salon located in Arjan, Dubai, offering professional hair, nail, skincare, lash, brow, hair-removal and massage services.

The redesigned homepage focuses on presenting Envy Salon as a welcoming, premium beauty destination. It aims to help women in Arjan and surrounding Dubai communities discover services, understand the salon's offering and make enquiries or appointments through WhatsApp, telephone and website booking options.

## 2. Main Improvements

### 1. Stronger Homepage Messaging and First Impression
- Replaced generic messaging with clearer ladies-only salon positioning.
- Introduced a more compelling headline and stronger visual direction.
- Prioritised premium salon imagery and prominent WhatsApp and service-discovery calls to action.

**Problem addressed:** The previous homepage did not immediately communicate the salon's identity, full service offering or reasons to choose Envy Salon.

### 2. Improved Service Discovery and Navigation
- Organised the salon's offering into clearer service categories.
- Made key treatments, including hair, nails, facials, massage, lashes and brows, easier to discover.
- Added direct service-discovery pathways to help visitors find relevant treatments.

**Problem addressed:** Visitors previously needed to navigate multiple service pages to understand the available treatments.

### 3. Stronger Trust Signals and Contact Visibility
- Improved the visibility of customer-rating information.
- Gave greater prominence to the salon team and professional expertise.
- Made contact and booking options easier to identify.
- Prioritised mobile-friendly Call and WhatsApp access.

**Problem addressed:** Important trust-building information and contact options were not sufficiently prominent for first-time visitors.

## 3. Design Improvements

The redesigned homepage establishes a more distinctive and polished visual identity through:

- **Visual presentation:** A more premium beauty-salon aesthetic supported by stronger imagery and clearer messaging.
- **Layout:** Better content organisation and more distinct homepage sections.
- **User experience:** Simpler service discovery and more accessible booking pathways.
- **Consistency:** Clearer visual hierarchy and more cohesive presentation across sections.
- **Mobile usability:** Greater emphasis on accessible contact and WhatsApp actions for mobile visitors.
